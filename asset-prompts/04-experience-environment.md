## Primary Prompt

> Premium athleisure lifestyle film, one continuous mood across four worlds,
> following young Indian athletes and creators (men and women, 20s–30s) wearing
> VAYU performance wear in black, graphite and soft blue-gray, no logos.
> World 1 — GYM: a minimal concrete training space at dawn, soft window light and
> haze; a lifter finishes a set and walks through the light, oversized pump cover
> swaying, breath visible in the cool air. World 2 — STREET: blue-hour city
> street in urban India (clean modern architecture, wet asphalt reflections); a
> figure in joggers and compression layer walks toward camera in smooth slow
> motion, fabric flowing with the stride. World 3 — STUDIO: dark editorial studio;
> a female athlete moves through a slow controlled stretch-to-flow sequence,
> activewear catching a silver-blue rim light, mist at floor level. World 4 —
> CREATOR: a rooftop at golden-blue dusk; a creator sets a camera, laughs, ties a
> hoodie at the waist — athleisure worn as life, not uniform. Camera:
> gimbal-smooth tracking and slow push-ins, 35mm/50mm, shallow depth,
> high-frame-rate moments on fabric motion. Grade: cinematic graphite blacks,
> muted silver highlights, soft blue-gray atmosphere, one restrained electric-blue
> accent in practical lights. Mood: fluid, aspirational, disciplined, breathable —
> luxury sportswear campaign, quietly confident, never aggressive.
> Photorealistic, natural diverse skin tones.
