# 01 — Clip 1: Hero Orbit

**Use:** Hero section motion swap (`#hero`) — replaces/underlays the master still.
**Format:** Video, 6–10s seamless loop, 4K 16:9 (plus 9:16 crop), 50–120 fps conformed to 24, no audio required.

---

## Primary Prompt

> Cinematic slow orbital shot, luxury athleisure campaign film: the camera circles
> smoothly (about 90° of arc, 15°/s) around an athletic Indian model standing in a
> dark minimal studio, wearing a matte-graphite VAYU performance outfit — fitted
> training top and tapered joggers, no logos. The model breathes slowly and shifts
> weight with calm control; the lightweight technical fabric ripples and floats a
> beat behind every micro-movement, hem and sleeves lifting as if in a gentle wind.
> Soft directional key light, a cool silver-blue rim light continuously sweeping
> along shoulders and fabric folds as the camera moves, fine mist drifting at floor
> level, soft reflections on a semi-gloss dark floor. Background: seamless graphite
> gradient with a faint blue-gray glow. Shallow depth of field, gimbal-smooth
> motion, shot at high frame rate so fabric motion feels suspended and airy.
> Palette: black, graphite, muted silver, soft blue-gray, restrained electric-blue
> rim. Mood: fluid, elegant, futuristic, breathable — premium fashion film, not a
> gym ad. Photorealistic fabric dynamics, natural skin.

## Loop & Motion Notes

- Start and end poses must match for a seamless loop (or shoot 180° and ping-pong).
- One hero motion only: the orbit + fabric. The model stays grounded — no jumping,
  no lifting, no posing transitions mid-clip.
- Fabric is the protagonist: ask for cloth-sim-quality drape and trailing motion.

## Negative / Avoid

- No fake logos or garment text. No lens flares, no speed ramps, no whip pans.
- No visible gym equipment; the space is an abstract studio void.

## Web Delivery

- H.265/H.264 MP4 ≤ 8 MB for hero autoplay (muted, `playsinline`, `loop`).
- Poster frame: match `00-master-hero-image` grade.
