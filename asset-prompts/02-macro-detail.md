## Primary Prompt

> Extreme macro product film, premium technical apparel: a slow sequence of
> ultra-close shots of VAYU performance fabric in graphite and muted silver.
> Beat 1 — raking light sweeps across a fine breathable knit, revealing the open
> airflow structure of the weave, individual threads crisp. Beat 2 — flatlock
> stitching in perfect parallel rows, camera drifting along the seam. Beat 3 — a
> matte zipper pull closing in silky slow motion, teeth meshing with precision.
> Beat 4 — fingers gently stretch the fabric diagonally; the knit elongates and
> then recovers its shape in slow motion, demonstrating four-way soft stretch.
> Beat 5 — the fabric releases and settles into an elegant floating drape, air
> visibly passing through as fine mist drifts behind the backlit textile.
> Lighting: hard raking edge light plus soft fill, cool silver-blue highlights on
> thread peaks, deep graphite shadow valleys, subtle haze. Extremely shallow depth
> of field, tripod-steady with millimetre-slow drift, high-frame-rate suspended
> motion. Palette: black, graphite, muted silver, soft blue-gray, faint
> electric-blue sheen. Mood: engineered, breathable, luxurious, precise.
> Photorealistic textile detail, no logos or labels visible.
