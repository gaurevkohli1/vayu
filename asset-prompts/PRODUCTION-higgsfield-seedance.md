## Delivery checklist (all video assets)

- Export master ProRes/4K, plus web MP4 (H.265, muted, ≤ 8 MB) for the site.
- Poster frames graded identically to the Master Hero image.
- 9:16 crops: keep the subject full-height, re-run rather than crop where possible.
- Reject any take with warped logo, invented text, garment morphing, or face
  drift between shots — the Identity Lock references are the ground truth.
