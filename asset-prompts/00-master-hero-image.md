# 00 — Master Hero Image

**Use:** Hero section background (`#hero`, `data-asset="00-master-hero-image"`).
**Format:** Still image. 16:9 master at 6K+ (crop to 4:5 and 9:16 for social). Also export a 1200×630 OG crop.

---

## Primary Prompt

> Premium athleisure campaign photograph: an athletic Indian model in their mid-20s
> standing in a vast minimal cinematic studio, wearing an elevated matte-graphite
> performance training set (fitted tee and tapered joggers, no visible logos).
> The model is caught in a subtle mid-motion turn — shoulders rotating, chin lifted,
> eyes calm — and the lightweight technical fabric is moving a half-second behind
> the body, hem and sleeve lifting softly as if touched by wind. Soft directional
> studio key light from camera left, a clean cool silver-blue rim light tracing the
> shoulder, jaw and fabric folds, raking highlights revealing fine knit texture.
> Thin atmospheric mist near the floor, airy lifted shadows, controlled semi-gloss
> floor reflection. Background: seamless graphite-to-black gradient with a faint
> soft blue-gray glow behind the figure. Color palette: black, graphite, muted
> silver, soft blue-gray, one restrained electric-blue accent in the rim light.
> Mood: fluid, elegant, futuristic, breathable, quietly powerful — luxury
> sportswear editorial, not aggressive gym advertising. Shot on 85mm, f/2.8,
> shallow depth, medium-full framing with generous negative space above and on the
> leading side of the motion. Hyper-detailed fabric, natural skin texture,
> photorealistic.

## Negative / Avoid

- No fake logos, brand marks, or lettering on garments (clean chest and thighs).
- No neon gym environment, no dumbbells in frame, no sweat spray, no screaming pose.
- No harsh HDR, no crushed blacks on fabric, no orange-teal blockbuster grade.

## Variants

1. **V1 — Male model,** oversized pump cover + joggers, fabric billowing on turn.
2. **V2 — Female model,** sculpted activewear set, arms mid-flow (dancer-adjacent).
3. **V3 — Duo,** staggered depth, one in focus, one soft — for wide crops.

- Deliver with and without mist pass if shooting composited.
