## Primary Prompt

> Cinematic design-studio montage, premium fashion documentary style: the making
> of a VAYU performance garment. Shot 1 — a designer's hand sketches a flowing
> athleisure silhouette in graphite pencil on off-white paper, loose elegant
> strokes, camera gliding over the desk in shallow focus; wind-curve motion lines
> visible in the sketch. Shot 2 — rolls and swatches of technical fabric in black,
> graphite and soft blue-gray arranged on a matte table; a hand lifts a swatch and
> lets it fall in slow motion, the textile floating down like air. Shot 3 — close
> on a sewing machine needle running a precise flatlock seam through graphite
> knit, threads catching a cool rim light, slow controlled push-in. Shot 4 — the
> finished garment on a minimal form in a dark studio, steam/mist drifting past,
> the silhouette lit by a single soft key and a silver-blue edge light.
> Environment: quiet atelier, graphite surfaces, warm-neutral task light mixed
> with cool ambient blue-gray, airy shadows, floating dust in light beams.
> Camera: gimbal-smooth drifts and push-ins, 35–50mm, shallow depth. Mood:
> disciplined craft, calm mastery, modern Indian design studio — elevated and
> minimal, never industrial or messy. Photorealistic hands and materials, no
> visible brand logos or text.
